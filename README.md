Duskline Cover Studio turns your photos into GTA 6-style key art for the fictional city of Solmar. Pick a photo, choose one of 6 layouts and 7 grades, keep every face safe with face-aware crops, and export the cover as PNG in three sizes (1:1, 4:5, 16:9) — all offline, right in your browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
